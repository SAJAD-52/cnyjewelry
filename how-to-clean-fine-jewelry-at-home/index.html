How to Clean Fine Jewelry at Home: Safe Care for Gold, Diamonds, and Gemstones

Discover professional yet gentle techniques to restore the brilliant sparkle of your solid gold, diamonds, and precious gemstones safely at home.

By McGee Jewelry · Updated 2026-11-22 · 5 min read

Why Proper At-Home Jewelry Cleaning Matters

Everyday lotions, perfumes, natural skin oils, and cooking residues create a microscopic film over fine jewelry. Over time, this buildup coats the pavilion and culet of diamonds, blocking light refraction and causing stones to look dull and cloudy. Regular, gentle cleaning preserves the mirror-like polish of solid gold and the breathtaking brilliance of your precious stones.

However, aggressive cleaning methods—such as harsh chemical dips, boiling water, or abrasive paper towels—can permanently damage precious metals and softer gemstones. Learning the correct, professional-grade home rituals ensures your investment pieces retain their showroom luster for generations.

Step-by-Step Guide to Cleaning Solid Gold and Diamonds

Solid 10k, 14k, and 18k gold paired with durable diamonds or platinum can withstand a simple, highly effective warm soap soak.

  1. Prepare the Cleaning Solution: Fill a small bowl with warm (not boiling) water and add a few drops of mild, phosphate-free dish soap.
  2. Soak the Pieces: Submerge your gold and diamond jewelry in the soapy bath for 15 to 20 minutes to loosen trapped oils and debris.
  3. Gently Brush: Use a ultra-soft baby toothbrush to gently scrub behind diamond settings and intricate chain links where residue accumulates.
  4. Rinse Thoroughly: Place your pieces in a strainer and rinse under warm running water. (Always plug your sink drain first!)
  5. Dry and Polish: Pat dry with a lint-free microfiber cloth or let air dry completely.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use toothpaste to clean solid gold jewelry?

No, toothpaste is abrasive and contains micro-silica particles that scratch polished gold and softer gemstones like pearls and opals. Always use mild dish soap and warm water.

How often should I clean my diamond engagement ring?

You should gently clean your diamond ring every one to two weeks with warm water, mild dish soap, and a soft-bristled baby toothbrush to remove accumulated lotion and skin oils.

Is ultrasonic cleaning safe for colored gemstones?

Ultrasonic cleaners vibrate at high frequencies and can crack heat-treated or fracture-filled gemstones such as emeralds, opals, tanzanite, and pearls. Stick to gentle manual soaking for colored stones.
