What Earrings Are Trending Now (Fall 2026)

Ears are getting louder this season. Here are the earring trends fashion and jewelry editors point to right now, how to wear them, and how to choose pieces that last past the season.

By McGee Jewelry · Updated October 2026

Oversized statement earrings are back

Fall and winter 2026 runway coverage points to a clear shift: supersized earrings, from ornate chandeliers and domed, ball-like shapes to floral drops and plush tassels. A related look borrows from the 1980s, with big, colorful, gold-framed earrings in round or square shapes, worn pierced or as clip-ons.

The way to wear them is to let them lead. Keep the neckline simple, pull your hair back, and skip a big necklace so the earrings are the focal point. Weight matters too: large earrings can pull on the lobe, so choose lightweight or hollow constructions for long wear, or try clip-ons. The earring finder shows which shapes suit your face.

Mixed metals and mismatched pairs

Matching pairs are no longer the rule. A popular, easy version pairs one dramatic drop on one ear with a small stud or huggie on the other, which gives contrast without buying two full sets. Mixing yellow gold, white gold and silver in the same ear is also current.

If mixing worries you, keep one metal dominant and add the second as an accent. The metal and undertone matcher helps you pick which tone should lead.

Baroque pearls, louder

Perfectly round pearl studs are giving way to irregular, organic baroque pearls, often oversized, worn as a single dramatic drop or as sized-up studs. Their uneven shapes read as modern rather than formal, and they suit both knits and evening wear.

Pearls are soft and dislike perfume, hairspray and soaking, so wipe them after wearing. See the care guide for pearls.

Sculptural gold with weight

Thick, domed hoops and sculptural studs in warm yellow gold are everywhere this fall. They have presence without sparkle, and they pair well with autumn colors. Solid gold of this size is costly, so many buyers choose hollow forms or gold vermeil. Our gold-plated jewelry guide explains what to expect, and the gold calculator shows how much real gold a piece contains.

Ear stacks, huggies and cuffs

If you prefer layers to one big statement, build a stack: a hoop, a huggie and a small stud on the same ear, mixing sizes and finishes. Ear cuffs add the same effect without piercings, since they clip onto the cartilage, and their 2026 versions favor organic shapes over sharp, industrial ones. Clip-on converters can also turn post earrings into clip-ons for comfort.

Color and gemstone drops

Moody autumn gemstones such as garnet, citrine, amber and deep green stones look best in drop earrings, where the color sits near the face. For ideas that go with the season, read our fall jewelry guide.

Choosing for your face and your ears

  • Face shape: use the earring finder. Round faces suit long, narrow drops, and longer faces suit wide hoops and studs.
  • Sensitive ears: look for implant-grade titanium, niobium, platinum or 14k gold and above, and avoid plated posts if you react to nickel.
  • Comfort: try the weight before you commit, and check that backs are secure.

Trend or staple? How to buy

Trends move quickly, so treat them as direction, not rules. Buy one trend piece, such as a statement drop or a baroque pearl, and build around staples you already wear daily: small hoops and studs. Check the cost per wear, and keep pieces you like in your wishlist. If you are buying for someone else, the gift finder can narrow ideas.

Frequently asked questions

What earrings are in style right now?

Oversized statement earrings, sculptural gold hoops, baroque pearls, mixed metals and ear stacks are the main directions this fall.

Are small studs out of style?

No. Small studs and hoops remain everyday staples. The trend is to add one bigger piece or combine them in a stack.

Are mismatched earrings in style?

Yes. Pairing a statement drop on one ear with a small stud or huggie on the other is a popular, easy way to wear the look.

Are clip-on earrings okay to wear?

Yes. Many oversized and 1980s-inspired styles come as clip-ons, and converters can turn post earrings into clip-ons for comfort.

Is gold or silver trending?

Warm yellow gold with weight leads this fall, but mixing gold and silver in the same ear is also current.
