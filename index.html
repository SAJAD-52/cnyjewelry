Questions, answered

How do I know my ring size at home?

Wrap a strip of paper around the base of your finger, mark the overlap, and measure it in millimeters. Match that to the circumference in the size guide. Measure at the end of the day, when fingers are warmest.

Which metal suits me best?

It depends on your undertone. Cool undertones tend to suit silver and white gold, warm ones suit yellow gold, and neutral skin suits both. The metal match tool shows gems too.

Can I wear my jewelry in the shower?

It is best not to. Soap film dulls stones, and water wears plating and weakens pearl strands. Solid gold and platinum tolerate it better than silver or plated pieces.

How accurate is the photo analyzer?

It reads color, so it gives a good estimate of the metal tone and any colored stone. Lighting and filters change results, and it cannot confirm a material. A jeweler can test it for certain.

Is my data saved or shared?

No. There are no accounts and no servers. Your wishlist is stored in your own browser, and uploaded photos never leave your device.
