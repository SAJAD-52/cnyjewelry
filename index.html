Questions & Security

Frequently asked

How do I know my ring size at home?

Wrap a strip of non-stretching paper around the base of your finger, mark where it overlaps, and measure the distance in millimeters. Match that circumference in our ring sizer at the end of the day when hands are warm.

How does Pro cloud sync protect my jewelry vault?

When signed in, pieces, wear logs, wishlist items, and compressed piece photos are encrypted and stored in your private Supabase vault with strict per-user row-level security. Logged-out sessions stay private on your device.

How is my jewelry box melt value calculated?

Melt value multiplies pure gold weight (grams × karat purity) by the benchmark spot rate, helping you track intrinsic bullion value separately from retail markup.
