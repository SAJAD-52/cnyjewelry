Jewelry to Wear This Fall: A Styling Guide

Fall changes how jewelry looks and how it has to behave: knits snag chains, necklines rise and the light turns warm. Here is a practical guide to the metals, stones and shapes that suit the season.

By McGee Jewelry · Updated October 2026

Lean into warm metals

Yellow gold and rose gold glow against autumn tones such as camel, burgundy and olive. Brushed and antique finishes add a lived-in richness that suits heavier fabrics. If you have a cool undertone, silver still works, especially with deep blues and grays. The metal and undertone matcher shows what flatters you.

Autumn gemstones

  • Garnet: deep red that reads like a wine glass in lamplight.
  • Citrine and amber: golden warmth.
  • Tiger’s eye and smoky quartz: earthy browns that pair with knits.
  • Emerald and peridot: forest and olive greens.
  • Opal and topaz: the birthstones for October and November.

Look up any month in the birthstone finder.

Make jewelry work with knitwear

Wool and cashmere snag fine, rough chains, so choose smooth links and secure clasps. With a turtleneck or high neck, wear chains long, about 70 to 90 cm, over the fabric rather than under it. Our layering guide shows lengths for every neckline. Chunky sweaters are a good moment for statement earrings and rings, since the neckline carries less detail.

The shapes of the season

  • Chunky chains and links that echo thick knits
  • Signet rings worn solo or stacked
  • Stacked thin rings in mixed tones
  • Textured or baroque pearls for a modern take on classic
  • Brooches on coats, scarves and lapels
  • Cuffs and bangles that sit over sleeves

Five easy outfit pairings

  1. Camel coat: gold hoops and a signet ring.
  2. Black turtleneck: a long gold chain and a pendant.
  3. Plaid shirt: a thin chain with a garnet pendant.
  4. Velvet dress: pearl drops and a slim cuff.
  5. Denim jacket: stacked rings and a brooch.

Fall practicalities

  • Cold fingers shrink. Rings can slip off. Measure when your hands are warm using the size guide.
  • Scarves tangle necklaces. Choose longer chains or fasten the clasp at the front.
  • Hand cream dulls stones. Apply it first, wait, then put rings on.
  • Store properly. Keep wool from rubbing against delicate pieces, with tips in the care guide.

A five-piece fall capsule

If you only add a few pieces, try gold hoops, a long chain, a signet or stackable ring, pearl earrings and a cuff. Save your picks in the wishlist, or let the gift finder help you buy for someone else.

Frequently asked questions

What metal is best for fall?

Yellow and rose gold are the classic choice for their warmth, though silver works well with cooler colors and undertones.

Can I wear silver in autumn?

Yes. Silver pairs well with navy, charcoal and burgundy, and mixing it with gold keeps the look current.

How do I stop chains snagging on sweaters?

Choose smooth links such as box or curb chains, keep clasps secure and wear necklaces over the sweater rather than under it.

Which birthstones belong to fall?

Common lists give opal for October and topaz or citrine for November, and sapphire is commonly given for September.
