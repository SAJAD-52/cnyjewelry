How to Measure Ring Size at Home: 4 Easy & Accurate Methods

Finding your exact ring size is the first step toward wearing comfortable, gorgeous jewelry. Here are 4 fast, reliable methods to measure your ring size at home.

By McGee Jewelry · Updated 2026-11-22 · 5 min read

Method 1: The Paper Strip or String Technique

The string or paper strip method is the quickest DIY option because it uses items you already have sitting around your house.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Cut a strip of paper or a piece of non-stretchy string roughly 3 to 4 inches long and 0.5 inches wide. (Paper is preferred over string, as string can stretch and give an inaccurate measurement.)
  2. Wrap the paper snugly around the base of the specific finger you plan to wear the ring on. Make sure it rests smoothly against your skin.
  3. Mark the exact spot where the paper overlaps with a pen or fine marker.
  4. Lay the paper flat next to a ruler with millimeter (mm) markings. Measure the length from the starting end to your pen mark. This value represents your finger's circumference.
  5. Compare your measurement to standard ring sizing charts (see table below).

> Pro Tip: Don't wrap the paper too tightly. A ring must glide comfortably over your knuckle while remaining secure enough not to slip off during daily activities.

---

Method 2: Measure an Existing Ring That Fits Perfectly

If you already own a ring that fits the target finger comfortably, measuring its diameter is one of the most reliable ways to find your correct size.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Select a ring that currently fits the exact finger you are sizing. Keep in mind that fingers on your dominant hand are often a half-size larger than those on your non-dominant hand.
  2. Use a ruler with millimeter markers to measure the inside diameter of the band. Place the ring flat on top of the ruler and measure the straight distance across the inner circle from inside edge to inside edge.
  3. Do not include the metal walls of the ring band in your measurement—measure only the open space inside.
  4. Match the millimeter inside diameter to a ring size conversion chart.

For instance, an inside diameter of 16.5 mm corresponds to a standard US Size 6. This method works exceptionally well when purchasing statement bands, such as our stunning 14k white and rose gold double diamond infinity design band.

Method 3: Use a Printable Ring Size Tool

Printable sizers offer a visual shortcut by giving you scaled standard circles or printable paper sizer belts.

How to Ensure Print Accuracy:

  • Check the Scale: Always ensure your printer setting is set to "100%" or "Actual Size" (do not select "Fit to Page").
  • Verify with a Coin: Most printable guides include a scale check bar or a circle designed to match a quarter or penny. Place the coin on top to confirm the scale is exact.
  • Place Your Ring Over the Circles: Move your existing ring along the chart until the inner edge of your ring aligns perfectly with the outer edge of the printed circle.

Method 4: Invest in an Inexpensive Plastic Ring Sizer Belt

If you plan to build a personalized stack from our complete collection of fine rings, investing in a plastic ring sizing belt or metal ring mandrel is a worthwhile choice.

These adjustable belt tools work like mini zip-ties. You thread the end through the buckle, slip it onto your finger, and adjust it until it feels like a comfortable ring fit. Read the size indicated by the arrow on the buckle. This tool removes all guesswork and is reusable for family and friends.

4 Crucial Tips for Accurate DIY Sizing

  1. Account for Knuckle Size: If your knuckle is significantly larger than the base of your finger, take two measurements—one at the knuckle and one at the base—and pick a size halfway between the two.
  2. Consider Temperature & Time of Day: Fingers shrink in cold weather and expand in heat or humidity. For the best accuracy, measure your fingers at the end of the day when they are warm and at their average size.
  3. Remember Band Width: Wider ring bands (above 5mm width) fit tighter than slim bands. If you are ordering a wide dome or multi-row band, consider sizing up by a half-size.
  4. Choose Quality Metals Carefully: Understanding metal durability helps you choose rings that hold their shape over time. Frequently asked questions

What is the average ring size for women and men?

The average ring size for women in the US ranges between Size 6 and Size 7. For men, the average size ranges between Size 9 and Size 10. However, everyone's hands are unique, so measuring your specific finger is always recommended.

What should I do if I am between sizes?

If your measurement falls between two sizes, we generally recommend sizing up to the larger size, especially if the ring features a wider band (over 4mm) or if your fingers tend to swell in warm weather.

Can wide-band rings affect my measured size?

Yes! Wide-band rings cover more surface area on your finger and create a snugger fit than thin, delicate bands. We recommend ordering a half-size larger than your normal size when purchasing wide bands.

How can I secretly measure someone else's ring size for a surprise?

The easiest way is to quietly borrow a ring they currently wear on that exact finger and measure its inner diameter using Method 2, or trace the inner circle onto a piece of paper to compare against a size chart later.
