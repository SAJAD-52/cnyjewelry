Spotlight: 14k White and Rose Gold Diamond Infinity Band (3/8 cttw)

Two metals, one endless line and a scatter of diamonds. Here is what to know about 14k white and rose gold infinity bands with about 3/8 carat of diamonds.

By McGee Jewelry · Updated October 2026

This is an editorial guide to the style, not a product listing. Specifications and prices vary by maker, so always confirm the details with the seller.

The design: two metals, one endless line

An infinity band uses a looping, figure-eight motif to suggest something without end, which is why it is popular for anniversaries and promise rings. In a two-tone version, white gold and rose gold weave through each other, with diamonds set along the curves. The contrast of cool white and warm pink makes the band flatter a wide range of skin tones, and lets it sit comfortably next to both silver and gold jewelry.

What 14k means for a ring

14k gold is 58.3% pure gold, alloyed with other metals for strength. That makes it harder than 18k, so it resists scratches better, which is why it is popular for rings worn daily. Rose gold gets its tint from copper. White gold is usually plated with rhodium for a bright, silvery finish. Compare karats in our gold guide.

What 3/8 cttw really tells you

“Cttw” means carat total weight: the combined weight of all the diamonds. Three-eighths of a carat is 0.375 ct. Spread across a band, that usually means many small stones, often around 1.5 to 2 mm each, rather than one large diamond. The total weight tells you about the diamond content, not how large the ring looks, so judge it by its width and pattern. The diamond size visualizer shows how sizes compare on a finger.

Judging small diamonds

  • Cut and sparkle. Look at the ring in person or in video. Well-cut small stones should flash evenly.
  • Color range. G to I looks white in white gold. Ask for the color range of the whole set.
  • Clarity range. Eye-clean grades such as SI are usually fine for small stones.
  • Matching. Stones should be uniform in size and tone.
  • Documentation. Small stones are rarely certified one by one. Ask for the total weight and grade ranges in writing.

Settings and comfort

Small diamonds are usually pavé, bead or channel set. Run a fingertip over the band: stones should be flush and smooth, without catching on sweaters or hair. Ask how deeply the metal grips each stone, since a few lost stones are the most common problem with pavé bands. Check the inside of the band for comfort, since a rounded edge sits better than a flat one.

Who it suits

  • Anniversary gift. The endless motif fits the occasion. See the anniversary gift guide.
  • Wedding or stacking band. Try it next to a solitaire, since the curves can leave small gaps.
  • Right-hand ring. A subtle sparkle that also works as a daily piece.

Sizing and resizing

Wider bands feel tighter, so many people go up about half a size. Measure with our ring sizer, ideally when your hands are warm. Check resizing before you buy: bands with stones set around much of the circle are hard or impossible to resize, and patterned designs can be distorted.

Care

Rhodium plating on white gold wears over time, and many owners have a jeweler re-plate it every year or two, depending on wear. Clean with warm soapy water and a soft brush, remove the ring for cleaning and heavy chores, and have the settings checked yearly. See the care guide for more.

Buying checklist

  1. Stamp: 14K on the band.
  2. Total diamond weight, color and clarity ranges, in writing.
  3. Whether the white gold is rhodium-plated, and how to maintain it.
  4. Resizing options and cost.
  5. Return, warranty and repair policy.
  6. Your ring size, measured twice.

Frequently asked questions

What does 3/8 cttw mean?

It means carat total weight of 0.375 ct, combined across all the diamonds in the ring.

Is 14k white gold the same as platinum?

No. White gold is gold alloyed with white metals and often rhodium-plated. Platinum is a different metal that is denser and naturally white.

Can a diamond infinity band be resized?

Sometimes, but designs with stones set around much of the band are hard to resize. Ask before you buy.

Does white gold need re-plating?

Often. The rhodium plating wears with time, and many jewelers re-plate it every year or two depending on wear.
