Spotlight: 14k Yellow Gold Necklace with a Devil Emoji Symbol

A tiny symbol with a big personality. Here is what to know about 14k yellow gold necklaces with a devil emoji pendant, from construction to chain length.

By McGee Jewelry · Updated October 2026

This is an editorial guide to the style, not a product listing. Specifications and prices vary by maker, so always confirm the details with the seller.

Emoji jewelry: why a devil charm?

Emoji jewelry turns the symbols we send every day into something you can wear. The smiling face with horns, usually called the devil or imp emoji, is the playful, cheeky one. It signals mischief, flirtation or a private joke, and that is exactly why it works as a pendant: small, graphic and full of personality.

Because devil imagery has different meanings for different people, think about who will see it. It suits a casual, humorous wardrobe well, and some people prefer a neutral symbol for family, work or religious settings.

Why 14k yellow gold suits a small pendant

14k gold is 58.3% pure gold, alloyed with other metals for strength. That makes it harder than 18k and better at holding the crisp edges of a small design, which matters when a pendant is only a centimeter or two across. Yellow gold also gives emoji shapes their familiar warm look. Compare karats in our gold guide, and if you are weighing a plated version instead, read the gold-plated jewelry guide.

Pendants see less friction than rings, so even fine detail tends to stay sharp for a long time with sensible care.

Choosing size and chain length

Pendant size sets the mood. Small pendants of around 8 to 10 mm read as delicate and work well layered, while larger ones of 12 to 15 mm and up make a clear statement worn on their own. As a starting point, an 16 to 18 inch (40 to 45 cm) chain places the pendant at the collarbone. Use our layering guide to see how lengths stack for your neckline.

A fine chain keeps the focus on the symbol, and a slightly heavier chain suits a larger pendant. Check that the bail, the small loop on the pendant, is thick enough for your chain.

How to style it

  • Keep it casual. It looks natural with tees, knits and denim.
  • Layer it. Pair it with a thin chain or a short pearl strand for contrast.
  • Let it be the joke. Wear it with otherwise simple jewelry so it stays the focal point.
  • Match your tone. Yellow gold flatters many skin tones, and the metal and undertone matcher shows what suits you.

Gifting a symbol with a story

Emoji pendants make good gifts for friends and partners because they carry a shared joke. Add a short note explaining why you chose it, and choose a friendlier emoji if you are unsure how the recipient will feel about devil imagery. The gift finder can help you narrow ideas by style and budget.

Caring for a small gold pendant

Fine detail traps soap, lotion and skin oils. Clean the pendant with warm water, a drop of mild soap and a soft brush, then dry it with a soft cloth. Keep it away from chlorine and take it off for workouts, and store it in its own pouch so the face does not scratch. More steps are in the care guide.

Buying checklist

  1. Stamp: look for 14K on the pendant or its bail.
  2. Solid or hollow: ask, because hollow pendants dent more easily.
  3. Weight: ask for the weight in grams, which tells you how much gold you are buying.
  4. Chain: check whether a chain is included and what it is made of.
  5. Finish: edges and the back should be smooth, with no rough spots.
  6. Design: ask whether it is the maker’s own artwork.
  7. Returns: confirm the policy before you buy.

Frequently asked questions

Is 14k yellow gold good for everyday pendants?

Yes. It is durable and holds detail well, and pendants see less wear than rings or bracelets.

Does a 14k gold pendant tarnish?

Gold itself does not tarnish like silver, though residue from lotions and skin oils can dull it. Regular gentle cleaning keeps it bright.

What chain length suits a small pendant?

A 16 to 18 inch (40 to 45 cm) chain places it near the collarbone, which suits most necklines.

Should I choose solid or hollow?

Solid is sturdier and holds more gold, while hollow looks larger for the price but is easier to dent. Ask the seller which it is.

Can I wear it every day?

Yes, if you remove it for workouts and swimming and clean it gently from time to time.
