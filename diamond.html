The 4Cs, simply

Cut

Drives sparkle more than anything else. Prioritize it first.

Color

Graded D to Z. Near-colorless (G to I) looks white in a setting and costs less.

Clarity

A grade that is clean to the eye, such as VS2 or SI1, usually looks the same as a higher one.

Carat

A measure of weight, not size. Just under a round number, such as 0.9 ct, often costs noticeably less.

Lab-grown or natural?

Lab-grown diamonds are real diamonds with the same chemistry and appearance, and they usually cost less. Natural stones tend to hold value better. Ask for a grading report from a recognized lab either way.
