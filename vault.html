Frequently Asked Questions

How is melt value calculated?

Melt value is the pure weight of precious metal multiplied by the live benchmark gold rate configured in the studio. It represents scrap metal intrinsic value without retail markups.

How is my collection data and photos secured?

When logged out, data is saved locally on your device. When logged in, your pieces and photos sync to your private Supabase cloud account, secured by Row Level Security policies readable only by you.

How do I unlock unlimited pieces?

Upgrade to McGee Jewelry Pro to store unlimited pieces across devices, track cleaning reminders, and generate printable insurance dossiers.
