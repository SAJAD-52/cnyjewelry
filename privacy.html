Privacy, Cloud Vault & Ads

How McGee Jewelry protects your collection data, handles subscriptions, and measures advertising.

Your Jewelry Data & Cloud Sync

When browsing as a guest, interactive calculations and photos analyzed in the Photo Matcher stay strictly on your device. When you create an account, your Jewelry Box items, compressed photos, Daily Ritual check-ins, and Size Passport sync securely to your private Supabase cloud account protected by strict Row Level Security.

Payments & Subscriptions

All McGee Jewelry Pro subscriptions are processed securely via Stripe. We never store your full credit card details on our servers, and you can manage or cancel your plan anytime in one click via the Stripe Billing Portal.

Analytics & Advertising Measurement

McGee Jewelry uses Google Analytics, Google AdSense, and optional Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel conversion events to measure studio engagement and campaign performance in Western & international markets. You may clear browser cookies or adjust ad preferences at any time.
